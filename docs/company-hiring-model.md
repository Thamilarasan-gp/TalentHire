# THAMILARASAN GLOBAL — Three-Sided Hiring Business Model

## 1. Core Operating Philosophy
**FIND. EVALUATE. HIRE.**

The platform bridges international companies seeking top Indian software engineering talent through a rigorous, transparent three-sided ecosystem:
1. **Companies**: B2B international hiring teams looking for pre-evaluated, production-ready engineers.
2. **Job Seekers / Software Engineers**: High-caliber developers who are vetted for free without paying application fees.
3. **Independent Expert Evaluators**: Principal and Staff Engineers paid unconditionally per completed technical assessment, independent of whether the candidate is ultimately hired.

## 2. Non-Negotiable Rules of Authority
- **The Evaluator NEVER makes the hiring decision.** Evaluators score rubric criteria (1-10) and issue recommendations (`PASS`, `FAIL`, `REVIEW_REQUIRED`). They never issue an offer or final hiring decision.
- **The Platform NEVER automatically hires.** There are no auto-hires or algorithmic placements.
- **The Company ALWAYS retains final decision authority.** The company conducts the final team fit interview and decides whether to extend an offer or decline.
- **Evaluator Financial Independence**: Evaluators receive fixed platform payouts upon QA approval of their scorecards. They receive zero commission on placements, eliminating perverse incentives to pass unqualified talent.

## 3. The Two Distinct Paths to Hire
| Feature | Path A: Quick Match | Path B: Decision Shortlist |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Value** | **Speed to Interview (1-2 Days)** | **Custom Depth & Specificity** |
| **Candidate Source** | Global Evaluated Talent Pool | Company Dedicated Requisition Pipeline |
| **Prior Evaluation** | Already completed & QA approved for platform | Newly commissioned 60-min live coding loop |
| **Evaluation Scope** | `REUSABLE` | `COMPANY_SPECIFIC` |
| **Action** | Immediate direct company interview | Multi-stage review, side-by-side comparison |
| **Re-evaluation** | Only if top-up required or expired | Always evaluated specifically for the role |
